Objective
Score points by rolling and banking before your luck runs out.

First to the target score triggers the last round — whoever's ahead when it ends wins.
🎲
Roll The Dice
Roll all six. Every die that can score glows bright — dull dice score nothing on their own.

Tap the bright ones you want to keep.
⚖️
Bank Or Push
Bank — lock in your points for the turn and pass the dice along.

Roll Again — re-roll what's left, adding to your total, but risking it all.
💥
Farkle
Roll nothing that scores and you farkle — every point from that turn is lost.

Your total from earlier turns is always safe.
🔥
Hot Dice
Score with all six dice in one turn and you get to roll all six again — same turn total, brand new roll.

It can keep happening again and again.
📊
Getting On The Board
Your very first turn needs to clear a minimum score before any points count toward your total.

Check Scoring Rules in the Advanced menu for the exact number — it's fully adjustable.
